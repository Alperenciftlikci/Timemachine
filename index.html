FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is my photo safe?
Yes. We don't sell or share your photos. Uploaded images are processed solely to generate your transformation and are not used to train AI models. Read more in our Privacy Policy.
How much does it cost?
New users get 3 free credits to try the app. After that, choose a weekly, monthly, or yearly subscription, or buy one-time credit packs. Yearly is our best value (60% off vs weekly).
Do you require an account?
No login screen, no email — you're anonymous from the first tap. Your credits and history sync via a private anonymous ID.
How long does each generation take?
Photos typically render in 15–45 seconds. Videos take 1–3 minutes depending on the model (Veo, Kling, or Sora). Pro subscribers get priority queue.
Can I cancel my subscription?
Yes — cancel anytime from your iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. No retention pressure, no hoops.
Does it work for pets too?
Yes — there's a dedicated Pets category. Age your dog/cat gracefully, see them as an Egyptian royal companion, and more.
