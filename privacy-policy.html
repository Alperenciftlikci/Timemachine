4. Photo Processing and AI Services

Important: Different AI providers handle photos and videos. Understanding how each works is crucial for your privacy.

4.1 Photo Generation (Google Firebase AI Logic / Gemini)

When you transform a photo into a different era or style:

  1. Upload: Your photo is securely transmitted via encrypted HTTPS directly from your device to Google's Firebase AI Logic service
  2. Processing: The Gemini 2.5 Flash Image model generates your transformed result
  3. Delivery: The generated image is returned directly to your device
  4. Retention: Google processes the image only to fulfill your request and does not use it to train models. Per Google's policy, input images are not retained beyond the duration of processing.

4.2 Video Generation (fal.ai / Seedance 2.0)

When you generate a time-travel video:

  1. Upload: Your photo is securely transmitted via HTTPS to our Firebase Cloud Functions
  2. Forward: The image is forwarded to fal.ai, which routes it to ByteDance's Seedance 2.0 video model
  3. Generation: The AI generates a short video clip from your image
  4. Delivery: The video URL is returned to your device
  5. Automatic Deletion: Your original uploaded photo is automatically deleted from fal.ai servers within 1 hour of processing completion

4.3 Our AI Partner Data Handling

Our AI processing partners (Google / Gemini and fal.ai / Seedance):

  • Process images only for the purpose of generating your requested content
  • Automatically delete uploaded inputs within 1 hour
  • Do NOT use your images to train AI models
  • Do NOT share your images with third parties
  • Maintain SOC 2 / ISO 27001 / equivalent enterprise security standards

4.4 Faces and Biometric Data

Although your photos may contain your face, the App uses your image only to generate the visual result you request. We do NOT perform facial recognition, we do NOT create, derive, or store any facial geometry, faceprint, or other biometric identifier, and we do NOT use your face to identify or track you. Your uploaded photo is processed transiently for generation and then deleted — it is never stored as a biometric template.

4.5 Generated Content Storage

  • Generation Metadata: Stored in our Firebase Firestore database (linked to your anonymous user ID) so that your gallery persists across sessions
  • Result Files: Photo results are served via the AI provider's secure CDN. Video results are served via our Cloudflare R2 storage
  • Local Save: You can optionally save any generated content to your device's photo library
